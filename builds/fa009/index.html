Defender 90

Military green against gloss black, short in the wheelbase and heavy in the stance.

Make & ModelLand Rover Defender 90Vehicle StatusVehicle Unavailable

Utility colours, treated as a design decision. Satin military green covers the body while gloss black takes the roof, bonnet, pillars and tailgate — a hard split rather than a blend, so the shape reads in two clear halves. Black wheels sit beneath black arch trims, and brushed steel steps are the only brightwork left.
