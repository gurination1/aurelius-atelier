0909How long we keep personal dataHow long we keep personal data

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including to satisfy legal, accounting, insurance and reporting requirements and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

When setting a retention period, we consider the amount, nature and sensitivity of the data, the risk of harm from unauthorised use or disclosure, the purpose of processing, whether that purpose can be achieved in another way, and applicable legal requirements.

In general: enquiries that do not lead to a transaction are kept for no longer than reasonably necessary to follow up and maintain an appropriate record of the enquiry; records connected with a quotation, booking, purchase or completed project may be kept for up to six years after the end of the relevant relationship, and longer where a legal, tax, insurance, warranty or dispute-related reason requires it; marketing information is kept until you opt out or we determine that it is no longer accurate or useful. We may retain minimal suppression information to ensure we honour an opt-out; cookie choices are kept for the period stated through the relevant cookie-control mechanism; and technical and security logs are kept for a limited period appropriate to troubleshooting, security and fraud prevention, unless a longer period is required to investigate an incident.

We may retain anonymised information that can no longer identify you for statistical or business-analysis purposes.

1010Cookies and similar technologiesCookies and similar technologies

The Website may use cookies, local storage, pixels, tags, scripts and similar technologies to make pages work, remember preferences, protect the Website, diagnose faults and understand how it is used.

Strictly necessary technologies may be used without consent where the law permits. Where a technology is not exempt from consent requirements, we will ask for consent before using it. Where the law permits a technology to be used for statistical or appearance purposes without consent, we will provide the required information and a simple way to object.

The Website's cookie-control mechanism, where displayed, provides current information about the non-essential technologies in use, their providers, purposes and duration, and allows you to accept, reject or change your choices. You can also restrict or delete cookies using your browser settings, although parts of the Website may not function correctly if strictly necessary storage is blocked.

We do not treat acceptance of these terms or continued browsing as consent to non-essential cookies.

1111How we protect personal dataHow we protect personal data

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure or access. These include access controls, appropriate service-provider contracts, system maintenance and security procedures proportionate to the nature of the information.

1212Your data-protection rightsYour data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to:

  • be informed about how we use your personal data;
  • ask for access to your personal data and obtain a copy;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data;
  • ask us to delete personal data;
  • ask us to restrict how personal data is used;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • object at any time to direct marketing;
  • receive certain personal data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format and ask for it to be transferred to another controller;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal; and
  • complain to us or to the Information Commissioner's Office.

These rights are not absolute and may not apply in every case. We may need to ask for information to verify your identity before acting on a request. We will not normally charge a fee, but the law permits a reasonable fee or refusal in limited circumstances.

Your right to object: You have an absolute right to object to the use of your personal data for direct marketing. You may also object to other processing based on our legitimate interests. To object, email bookings@wrpdgroup.com.

1313ComplaintsComplaints

You have the right to complain to us if you believe we have infringed data-protection law in the way we handle your personal data.

You can make a complaint by emailing bookings@wrpdgroup.com, using any electronic privacy or complaint form made available on the Website, or writing to our registered office using the contact details in section 1. Please explain what you believe has gone wrong and provide enough information for us to investigate.

We will acknowledge a data-protection complaint within 30 days, take appropriate steps to investigate it, and communicate the outcome without undue delay. We may contact you if we need further information.

You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). Its contact details and complaints service are available at https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/. We would appreciate the opportunity to address your concern first, but you are not required to contact us before approaching the ICO where the law allows.
