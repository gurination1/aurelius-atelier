Aurelius Atelier Security & Privacy

Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 | GDPR & CCPA Fully Compliant

01 Zero-Vendor Data Leaks & Information Collection

Aurelius Atelier maintains the highest standards of discretion and confidential client handling. We collect only the information necessary to fulfill your automotive bespoke commissions and respond to build inquiries.

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, phone number, secure email address, and delivery location.
  • Vehicle Telemetry & VIN: Make, model, year, chassis VIN, and current mechanical specifications.
  • Zero Third-Party Ad Trackers: Our digital platform operates with zero marketing surveillance tags, zero Facebook/Meta pixels, and zero data brokering.

02 Use of Information

Collected information is used exclusively for:

  • Engineering bespoke build sheets and component sizing.
  • Facilitating direct concierge communication and workshop progress updates.
  • Executing secure wire transfers and vehicle title transfers.

03 Data Subject Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Clients and partners possess the right to access, rectify, or request complete deletion of their personal records at any time. Inquiries may be directed to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@aureliusatelier.co.uk.
